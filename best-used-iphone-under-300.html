Best used iPhone under $300

Five used iPhones that sell for under $300 right now, and which one suits you.

Prices as of November 2026

Some links on this page may earn us a commission if you buy. It never changes the price you pay or which phones we list.

The short answer

Get a used iPhone 13. It sells for about $245 with 128GB and still has the same A15 Bionic chip as the iPhone 14. If you can stretch to the top of your budget, a 128GB iPhone 14 adds safety features. If you want to spend as little as possible, look at the iPhone 12 or the iPhone SE (3rd generation).

All prices below are Swappa's average sale prices as of October 7, 2026. A range covers different storage sizes, so the low end is usually the smallest storage. Only the storage sizes priced under $300 fit this budget.

What about the others?

iPhone 13 Pro starts at $294, so only the smallest storage size squeezes under $300. iPhone 11 is cheap ($138–$207), but Swappa notes it and the iPhone SE (2nd generation) are the oldest iPhones on Apple's iOS 27 list, so they're the most exposed when Apple announces the next version's support at WWDC in June 2027. At this budget, a newer phone is the safer bet.

Before you buy

Check battery health in Settings > Battery > Battery Health. Swappa sets no minimum, so ask the seller for the Maximum Capacity figure. To confirm a phone is unlocked, the Carrier Lock line on the About screen should say "No SIM restrictions". Our pre-purchase checklist covers the IMEI check, Activation Lock and safe payment.
