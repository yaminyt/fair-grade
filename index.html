Buying guides

    Before you pay for any used phone

    1. Check the IMEIDial *#06# and run the number through a lost-or-stolen check. A blacklisted phone won't connect to a carrier.
    2. Confirm it's unlockedActivation Lock (iPhone) or Google account lock (Android) must be off, or the phone is a paperweight.
    3. Ask for battery healthBelow 80% usually means a replacement soon. Factor that into the price.
    4. Pay with protectionUse a marketplace or card with buyer protection. Never pay by gift card or wire.
